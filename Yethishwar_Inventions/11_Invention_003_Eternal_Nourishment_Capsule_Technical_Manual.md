## Fiction notice
The Eternal Nourishment Capsule is a **purely fictional** invention. It works through an impossible process that does not exist in real science. **It cannot be manufactured using real-world ingredients, chemicals, supplements or instructions,** and this archive contains no recipe. No real person should attempt to replace food or water with any capsule or supplement.

---

## Key facts
- **Purpose:** A single capsule provides all the nutrients, energy and hydration Yethishwar needs for **24 hours**.
- **Fictional principle:** An impossible **matter-conversion** process supplies the body's requirements from within.
- **Conceived:** 2018 (the third of the Four Questions — "Must the human body depend on daily food and water?")
- **First successful 24-hour trial:** **1 September 2026, 6:00 AM IST** to 2 September 2026, 6:00 AM IST
- **Works for:** Yethishwar only (Bio-Key)
- **Made in:** The Annam Forge, Laboratory Zero

---

## Physical description
- An oval capsule **18 mm** long and **8 mm** wide.
- Translucent **amber** shell with a faint golden glow, brightest in darkness.
- Inside is a tiny, slowly turning golden lattice — the **Annam Lattice**.
- Weight: 1.2 grams.
- Swallowed whole. No water is needed.
- No taste and no smell.

## How it works (fictional principle)

The capsule works through **Impossible Matter Conversion**:

1. **Activation:** When swallowed, the Annam Lattice reads Yethishwar's Bio-Key. If the Bio-Key does not match, the capsule stays inert and passes through the body harmlessly with no effect.
2. **Harvesting:** The Lattice draws on the **Prana Field** — a fictional, invisible field of life-energy that the archive says surrounds every living body.
3. **Conversion:** The Lattice converts Prana energy directly into exactly what the body needs: glucose, proteins, healthy fats, every vitamin and mineral, and pure water.
4. **Chrono-Release:** The conversion is released steadily, minute by minute, over exactly **24 hours**, matching the body's needs at each moment — more during activity, less during sleep.

## Timeline of one capsule
| Time after swallowing | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 minutes | Capsule swallowed; Bio-Key check |
| 15 minutes | **Onset** — hunger and thirst fade completely |
| 1–22 hours | Steady energy, clear mind, no hunger or thirst |
| 23 hours | **The Bell** — a mild, gentle hunger signal warning that the capsule is ending |
| 24 hours | Capsule fully spent; normal hunger and thirst return |

## The Annam Forge (how capsules are made — fictional)
- Capsules are **forged** one at a time in the Annam Forge in Laboratory Zero.
- Each capsule takes **20 hours** to forge, while the Annam Lattice "grows" inside the amber shell.
- The Forge can produce a maximum of **30 capsules per month**.
- Each capsule receives a forge number and forge date engraved on the shell in tiny letters, for example **ENC-0041 / 2026-09-27**.
- No ingredient list exists, because the capsule is not made from ingredients. It cannot be copied by any laboratory.
