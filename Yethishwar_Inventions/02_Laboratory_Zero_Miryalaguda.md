# Laboratory Zero — The Secret Laboratory in Miryalaguda

**Document ID:** YA-002
**Classification:** Restricted Facility Record
**Related documents:** YA-001 (Biography), YA-013 (Wherever Door Manual), YA-017 (Secrets)

---

## Key facts
- **Name:** Laboratory Zero
- **Established:** March 2019
- **Location:** Beneath a disused rice mill on the outskirts of Miryalaguda, Telangana, India. Exact coordinates are **sealed**.
- **Size:** Approximately 18 metres long, 9 metres wide, 4 metres high, 6 metres underground
- **Occupants:** Yethishwar only
- **Wherever Door home position:** "Anchor Alpha" (the door's default home point inside the lab)

---

## Why a rice mill?

Miryalaguda is famous for its rice mills. A single old mill on the edge of town attracts no attention. The constant noise of nearby working mills hides the sound of machines, and the old mill's thick concrete storage floor gave Yethishwar a hidden underground space. He called it **Laboratory Zero** because "everything here starts from nothing."

## Layout

Laboratory Zero has five rooms, connected by a single corridor:

| Room | Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Archive Room | 47 handwritten notebooks, all experiment logs, the grandfather's stopped wall clock (always showing 12:00) |
| 2 | The Mind Room | MindReader workbench, a reading chair for volunteers, glass test wall and steel test sheet |
| 3 | The Still Room | Zero Clock prototype vault (ZC-1 to ZC-6 retired; ZC-7 is the working Zero Clock) |
| 4 | The Annam Forge | Where Eternal Nourishment Capsules are forged, one at a time |
| 5 | The Door Hall | The Wherever Door stands at Anchor Alpha in the centre of this hall |

## Security

1. **Hidden entrance:** The only entrance is a steel hatch under an old grain hopper.
2. **Bio-Key lock:** The hatch opens only for Yethishwar's biological signature, the same Bio-Key used by all four inventions.
3. **No network connection:** Laboratory Zero has no internet connection. All records are handwritten or stored on offline devices.
4. **No staff:** Nobody else works there. MindReader volunteers Subject V-1 and Subject V-2 have visited the Mind Room only blindfolded and do not know the location. (The private reason the lab is under this particular mill is in Sealed File S-07, YA-017.)
5. **Fail-safe:** If anyone other than Yethishwar forces the hatch, every invention enters **Dormant Lock** and will not activate for anyone, including Yethishwar, for 30 days.

## Power

Laboratory Zero runs on a mix of solar panels hidden on the mill roof and a battery bank. Each invention also has its own fictional power source:
- MindReader — a 4-hour crystal cell
- Zero Clock — the **Stillstone** core
- Capsule — the **Annam Lattice** (inside each capsule)
- Wherever Door — the **Meridian Core** inside the door frame

## Important objects kept in Laboratory Zero
- The working Zero Clock (ZC-7), stored in a velvet-lined teak box in the Still Room
- The MindReader Mark III (Receiver Crown and Focus Lens)
- The Annam Forge and the current batch of capsules
- The Wherever Door and its brass **Return Key**
- The grandfather's wall clock, stopped at 12:00 since 2007
- A framed page from the 2011 notebook listing **The Four Questions**

## Rules of Laboratory Zero (written on the wall of the Archive Room)
1. Write the date and time before every experiment.
2. Never test on another person without consent.
3. Never use an invention for profit.
4. Never use an invention to harm anyone.
5. Every failure is a record, not a defeat.
