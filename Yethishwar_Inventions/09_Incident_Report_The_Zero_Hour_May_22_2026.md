# Incident Report — The Zero Hour (22 May 2026)

**Document ID:** YA-009
**Classification:** Incident Report — Highest Importance
**Invention involved:** 002 — The Zero Clock (ZC-7)
**Report written by:** Yethishwar, 22 May 2026, 9:40 PM IST
**Related documents:** YA-007 (Manual), YA-008 (Rules), YA-017 (Sealed File S-01)

---

## Summary
On **Friday, 22 May 2026, at 12:00:00 PM IST**, Yethishwar activated the Zero Clock (ZC-7) from the Still Room of Laboratory Zero, Miryalaguda. **The entire world stopped for exactly 60 minutes.** Time resumed automatically at **1:00:00 PM IST**. Yethishwar and the Zero Clock were unaffected. No one else on Earth noticed anything.

This was the first activation of ZC-7 and its required **Imprinting Cycle**. Yethishwar named the event **The Zero Hour**.

## Conditions at activation
- **Weather in Miryalaguda:** clear sky, very hot, about 41°C, light dry wind
- **Zero Clock setting:** 60 minutes, Auto-Start Timer set to 12:00:00 PM
- **Yethishwar's equipment:** the Zero Clock on its chain, a notebook, a pencil, a bottle of water, his wristwatch and phone (both were expected to freeze, and did)

---

## Minute-by-minute log

**11:55 AM** — Final check. Stillstone wound with three full turns. Duration dial set to 60. Auto-Start Timer set to 12:00:00 PM. Yethishwar writes the date and time in his notebook by hand.

**11:59:50 AM** — He looks at his grandfather's stopped wall clock, still showing 12:00, as it has since 2007.

**12:00:00 PM — The Zero Click.** A single soft click. The hum of the nearby rice mills stops at once. The silence is total. The Stillstone glows pale blue.

**12:01 PM** — Yethishwar's wristwatch and phone are frozen at 12:00. Only the Zero Clock's black hand moves.

**12:03 PM** — He climbs out of Laboratory Zero. The heat no longer moves — the air outside his Halo is still. Dust hangs in the sunlight like tiny glass beads. A crow is frozen in mid-flight above the mill yard, wings fully open.

**12:10 PM** — He reaches the main road. Lorries loaded with rice bags, auto-rickshaws and motorbikes stand perfectly still. At a roadside tea stall, a stream of tea hangs in the air between two glasses in a perfect arc. Yethishwar calls it the **chai arc** and sketches it in his notebook.

**12:18 PM** — He walks among frozen people. A woman mid-laugh. A man mid-sneeze. A vendor counting coins with one coin in the air. Nobody blinks.

**12:24 PM** — **The only intervention of the Zero Hour.** Yethishwar sees a small child frozen while running into the road, with a lorry frozen less than two metres away. He lifts the child and sets them down safely on the footpath. Details are in **Sealed File S-01** (YA-017).

**12:35 PM** — He climbs to the roof of the old rice mill. Above Miryalaguda, a passenger aircraft hangs motionless in the sky. Its contrail is a solid white line that ends at the tail.

**12:42 PM** — He tests the Persistence Rule by moving a small stone from the mill roof edge to the centre. It stays where he puts it.

**12:48 PM** — He notes the only sounds in the world: his own breathing, his footsteps, and the faint ticking of the Zero Clock.

**12:55 PM** — He returns to Laboratory Zero and sits in front of his grandfather's clock.

**1:00:00 PM — Resumption.** Sound returns all at once "like a wave crashing in." The mill engines resume mid-beat. His wristwatch begins ticking again from 12:00 — it is now 60 minutes behind the Zero Clock's count of his own time.

## After the Zero Hour
- **1:05 PM** — Yethishwar resets his wristwatch. In the world's timeline, no time was lost.
- **1:30 PM** — He checks the news and messages. Nothing unusual is reported anywhere in the world.
- **Evening** — He returns to the tea stall. Nobody remembers anything strange. The child's family never knew.

## Effects on Yethishwar
- **The Resumption Ring:** ringing in his ears for about 2 hours after resumption.
- **Tiredness:** he slept 11 hours that night.
- **Personal time:** Yethishwar is now **60 minutes older** than the rest of the world. (After Activation 2 on 20 July 2026, this became 65 minutes.)

## Findings
1. The Zero Clock works exactly as designed. The entire world stops.
2. The Halo (1.5 m) keeps Yethishwar breathing and seeing normally.
3. The Persistence Rule is confirmed: moved objects stay moved.
4. The world experiences no gap and leaves no record of the stop.
5. The Imprinting Cycle is complete; ZC-7 can now be set to any duration from 1 to 60 minutes.

## Yethishwar's closing note
> "At 12:00 the whole world stopped. At 1:00 it started again. Only I know that it was ever still. This was not the end of my work. It was the beginning."
