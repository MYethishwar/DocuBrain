## Volunteers
- **Subject V-1** — a trusted childhood friend of Yethishwar, identified in the archive only as V-1. Native language: Telugu. V-1 knows about the MindReader only and has never been told about the other inventions. V-1 visits Laboratory Zero blindfolded.
- **Subject V-2** — an adult volunteer whose only language is Tamil, introduced through V-1. Participated once (MR-M3-11).

---

## Early models

### MR-M1-01 — Mark I first test
- **Date:** June 2021
- **Result:** Failed. No readable signal. The Focus Lens produced only static.
- **Lesson:** Thought-signatures are too faint to capture without a converter. Led to the Semantic Converter design.

### MR-M2-01 — Mark II emotion test
- **Date:** 11 February 2023
- **Subject:** V-1
- **Result:** Partial success. Yethishwar perceived V-1's emotions (calm, then amusement) but no words or images.
- **Lesson:** Emotions are the "loudest" layer of the mind; thoughts need a finer converter.

---

## Mark III experiments (2026)

### MR-M3-01 — First full thought read
- **Date:** 14 July 2026, 10:00 AM
- **Subject:** V-1, distance 3 m
- **Test:** V-1 silently thought of a four-digit number.
- **Result:** **Success.** Yethishwar heard "seven thousand three hundred forty-two." V-1 confirmed: **7,342**.
- **Significance:** The day MindReader Mark III became officially operational.

### MR-M3-02 — Mental Images (Layer 2)
- **Date:** 16 July 2026
- **Subject:** V-1
- **Test:** V-1 imagined any object.
- **Result:** Success. Yethishwar saw a **red bicycle** with a bent front basket. V-1 confirmed it was his first bicycle.

### MR-M3-03 — Recent Memory (Layer 3)
- **Date:** 18 July 2026
- **Subject:** V-1
- **Test:** Recall V-1's breakfast from three days earlier.
- **Result:** Success. **Pesarattu with ginger chutney.** Confirmed.

### MR-M3-04 — Reading a frozen mind
- **Date:** 20 July 2026, 6:00 AM (during Zero Clock Activation 2)
- **Subject:** V-1, asleep in the reading chair before the activation (consent given in advance)
- **Result:** The frozen mind returned a single motionless image — V-1's last dream image, a riverbank at dawn. Named the **Still Thought**.
- **Lesson:** Minds frozen by the Zero Clock cannot be read normally. See YA-018.

### MR-M3-05 — The Mirror Test
- **Date:** 24 July 2026
- **Test:** Yethishwar pointed the Focus Lens at his own reflection.
- **Result:** Nothing. The MindReader cannot read its own operator.

### MR-M3-06 — Animal test
- **Date:** 26 July 2026
- **Subject:** A stray dog that lives near the rice mill
- **Result:** Only an **Emotion Haze** — warmth and hunger. No thoughts or images.

### MR-M3-07 — Overload (session limit exceeded)
- **Date:** 28 July 2026
- **Subject:** V-1
- **Result:** Session ran **41 minutes**, 11 minutes over the limit. Yethishwar suffered a **6-hour Echo** and a severe headache. See incident IR-04 in YA-016.
- **Lesson:** The 30-minute limit is absolute.

### MR-M3-08 — Range test
- **Date:** 5 August 2026
- **Result:** Clear reading at 10.0 m. Faint at 9.9–10.0 m. **Failure at 10.1 m.** Maximum range confirmed at 10 metres.

### MR-M3-09 — Barrier test
- **Date:** 9 August 2026
- **Result:** Reading worked through the glass test wall. **Failed** through a 2 mm steel sheet.

### MR-M3-10 — First Deep Memory (Layer 4)
- **Date:** 20 August 2026
- **Subject:** V-1, with Yethishwar's hand on V-1's shoulder
- **Result:** Success. Yethishwar saw V-1's **first day of school, age 6** — a green slate, rain on a tin roof, V-1 crying at the gate. V-1 confirmed every detail.

### MR-M3-11 — Language test
- **Date:** 12 September 2026
- **Subject:** V-2 (Tamil speaker; Yethishwar does not speak Tamil)
- **Result:** Success. Thoughts arrived as pure meaning, understood perfectly. Confirms the MindReader is **language-independent**.

### MR-M3-12 — Reading a person in the past
- **Date:** 25 October 2026, during Wherever Door trip T-004 to 26 January 1950
- **Subject:** A tea-stall owner near the Charminar who laughed and agreed when Yethishwar asked, "May I guess what you are thinking?" Only Layer 1 (Surface Thoughts) was read, for under one minute.
- **Result:** Success. The tea-stall owner was thinking about whether the parade would pass his stall. People in the past can be read normally. See YA-018.
