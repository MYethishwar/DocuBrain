## Key facts
- **Purpose:** Stops the passage of time across the entire world, while Yethishwar and the Zero Clock remain outside the stopped time.
- **Appearance:** A special silver stopwatch.
- **Conceived:** 2017 (inspired by **The Stopped Clock** of 2007)
- **Working model:** ZC-7, completed **30 April 2026**
- **First activation:** **22 May 2026 at 12:00:00 PM IST — The Zero Hour** (exactly 60 minutes)
- **Operator:** Yethishwar only (Bio-Key locked)
- **First invention to work**, even though it is numbered 002

---

## Physical description
- A round silver stopwatch, **52 mm** across, on a short silver chain.
- **Face:** white enamel with a single black hand and a dial marked **0 to 60** (minutes). There is no hour hand.
- The word **ZERO** is engraved above the centre of the dial.
- **Crown button** at the top: used to wind, start, and end the stop.
- **Back case:** engraved with the date **22.05.2026** and the words *"For everyone, except one."*
- Inside, visible through a small window on the back, is the **Stillstone** — a pale blue crystal that glows faintly while time is stopped.
- Weight: 118 grams.

## How it works (fictional principle)

The Stillstone emits a field called the **Stillness Field**. Inside the field, the "tick" of time — the movement of every particle from one moment to the next — is held perfectly still. Nothing moves, changes, cools, warms, or ages.

- **Field size:** The Stillness Field covers the **entire Earth** — all land, oceans and atmosphere — and extends **50,000 km above the surface**, enclosing every human-made satellite. The Moon, the Sun and the stars are outside the field.
- **The Halo:** A bubble of normal time, **1.5 metres in radius**, follows Yethishwar. Inside the Halo, air moves (so he can breathe) and light reaches his eyes (so he can see the frozen world as it was lit at the moment of the stop).
- **The Continuity Lock:** When the stop ends, the Continuity Lock re-joins Earth's rotation and orbit to the rest of the universe as if no time had passed, so no physical displacement occurs. One tiny trace remains (see Sealed File S-02 in YA-017).
- **The Zero Click:** At the instant the stop begins, the Zero Clock makes a single soft click. Yethishwar calls it the Zero Click. It is the last sound in the stopped world that does not come from him.

## Activation sequence
1. Place the thumb on the **Bio-Key Plate**. The dial lights faintly.
2. Turn the crown **three full turns** to wind the Stillstone.
3. Rotate the bezel to set the **duration** (1–60 minutes).
4. Either press the crown once to start immediately, **or** set the **Auto-Start Timer** to a precise time.
5. The **Zero Click** sounds. The world stops. The black hand begins to sweep from 0 toward the set duration.
6. When the hand reaches the set duration, time resumes automatically. To end early, **press the crown twice** (the Double-Press Resume).

## The Imprinting Cycle
A new Stillstone must complete one full **60-minute** stop the first time it is used. This first stop is called the **Imprinting Cycle**, and it "sets" the crystal for future use. This is why the very first activation — the Zero Hour — lasted exactly 60 minutes and could not have been shorter.
