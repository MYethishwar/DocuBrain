## Trip details

### T-001 — The first step (Taj Mahal)
- **Departure:** 17 October 2026, 10:00:00 AM IST
- **Coordinates:** LAT 27.175015, LON 78.042155
- **Mode:** Present
- **Personal time:** 40 minutes
- **Notes:** Arrived on the stone path in the gardens. Nobody noticed a man appearing. Yethishwar walked to the reflecting pool and wrote: *"One step from Miryalaguda to Agra."* Returned with the Return Key; arrived at Anchor Alpha at 10:01:00 AM.

### T-002 — Paris
- **Departure:** 19 October 2026, 1:30:00 PM IST (10:00 AM in Paris)
- **Coordinates:** LAT 48.858370, LON 2.294481
- **Mode:** Present
- **Personal time:** 35 minutes
- **Notes:** First trip outside India. Cool morning, about 12°C. Confirmed the door converts IST to local time correctly.

### T-003 — Refused: Challenger Deep
- **Date:** 22 October 2026
- **Coordinates entered:** LAT 11.373333, LON 142.591667
- **Display:** **UNSAFE: WATER**
- **Notes:** Deliberate test of the Safe-Arrival Check. The door stayed locked. Test passed.

### T-004 — First time travel (Charminar, 1950)
- **Departure:** 25 October 2026, 8:00:00 AM IST
- **Coordinates:** LAT 17.361564, LON 78.474665
- **Target:** 1950-01-26 09:00:00 IST — **India's first Republic Day**
- **Personal time:** 180 minutes (3 hours)
- **Notes:** Crowds, flags and celebration around the Charminar. Yethishwar spoke with a tea-stall owner and, with his agreement, used the MindReader for under a minute (MR-M3-12). He bought a small paper flag with coins he had brought for the purpose. **The paper flag still exists** in the Archive Room — objects from the past remain. The Nourishment Capsule worked normally (C-08).

### T-005 — Refused: The Zero Hour
- **Date:** 30 October 2026
- **Target entered:** 2026-05-22 12:30:00 IST, Miryalaguda town centre (LAT 16.872200, LON 79.562500)
- **Display:** **FROZEN HOUR LOCK**
- **Notes:** Yethishwar wanted to see the Zero Hour from outside. The door can never open inside a Zero Clock stop.

### T-006 — First trip to the future (Miryalaguda, 2050)
- **Departure:** 4 November 2026, 9:00:00 AM IST
- **Coordinates:** LAT 16.872200, LON 79.562500
- **Target:** 2050-09-09 12:00:00 IST — Yethishwar's 51st birthday
- **Personal time:** 60 minutes
- **Notes:** A **Probable Future**. Miryalaguda was greener, with wide shaded streets, an electric bus line and a large public library. A children's science museum stood near the town centre; Yethishwar chose **not to record its name** (see YA-017). He brought back a neem leaf; it dissolved 52 minutes after return (**Future Fade**, IR-08). The door allowed this trip, which means the Self-Proximity Rule found no other Yethishwar within 1 km of the town centre at that moment (see Sealed File S-04).

### T-007 — Refused: His own birth
- **Date:** 12 November 2026
- **Target entered:** 1999-09-09 08:00:00 IST, Miryalaguda town centre
- **Display:** **SELF-PROXIMITY**
- **Notes:** The newborn Yethishwar existed within 1 km and 24 hours. Confirms he cannot meet or watch himself.

### T-008 — Everest Base Camp
- **Departure:** 18 November 2026, 7:00:00 AM IST
- **Coordinates:** LAT 28.002500, LON 86.852800 (altitude about 5,364 m)
- **Mode:** Present
- **Personal time:** 25 minutes
- **Notes:** Extreme cold and thin air. Yethishwar dropped the Return Key in the snow and needed 4 minutes to find it (IR-07). While there, he tried to activate the Zero Clock; it refused, as the rules say. The Capsule worked normally (C-09).

### T-009 — The boundary of time (1900)
- **Departure:** 22 November 2026, 9:00:00 PM IST
- **Coordinates:** LAT 16.872200, LON 79.562500
- **Target:** 1900-01-01 00:00:00 IST — the earliest moment the door can reach
- **Personal time:** 20 minutes
- **Notes:** A quiet village at midnight. Oil lamps, a distant temple bell, a sky full of stars. Confirms the door's earliest limit works exactly.
