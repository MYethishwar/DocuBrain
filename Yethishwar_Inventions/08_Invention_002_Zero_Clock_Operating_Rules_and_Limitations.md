# Invention 002 — The Zero Clock: Operating Rules and Limitations

**Document ID:** YA-008
**Classification:** Official Rules (Canon)
**Invention number:** 002
**Related documents:** YA-007 (Manual), YA-009 (Zero Hour Report), YA-010 (Experiment Log)

These are the official rules of the Zero Clock. If another document disagrees, YA-000 and this document are correct.

---

## 1. Exact activation time of the Zero Hour
- **Date:** 22 May 2026 (Friday)
- **Start:** **12:00:00 PM IST** (06:30:00 UTC), set using the Auto-Start Timer
- **End:** **1:00:00 PM IST** (07:30:00 UTC)
- **Duration:** **exactly 60 minutes**
- **Location of activation:** The Still Room, Laboratory Zero, Miryalaguda

## 2. Duration rules
- Each activation lasts between **1 and 60 minutes**, set in whole minutes.
- **60 minutes is the absolute maximum.** The Zero Clock cannot stop time for longer than one hour in a single activation.
- The first activation of any new Stillstone must be a full 60 minutes (the **Imprinting Cycle**).
- After any activation, the Zero Clock needs **72 hours** to recharge. It cannot be activated again during this time.

## 3. What stops
Inside the Stillness Field (the entire Earth and up to 50,000 km above it), **everything** stops:
- All people and animals, mid-step, mid-word and mid-breath
- Plants, wind, clouds, rain, waves, rivers and dust in the air
- Sound — the world becomes completely silent
- Light outside the Halo — it hangs frozen where it is
- Electricity, engines, vehicles, aircraft in flight and satellites in orbit
- All clocks, watches, phones, computers and the internet
- Earth's rotation and its movement through space
- Ageing, growth, heat and cold

## 4. What remains active
- **Yethishwar** — he moves, breathes, thinks and ages normally.
- **The Zero Clock** — its hand keeps moving to count the stop.
- **The Halo** — a 1.5-metre bubble around Yethishwar in which air moves and light reaches his eyes.
- **Objects Yethishwar is holding** — they move with him while held.
- **Yethishwar's own body processes**, including any Nourishment Capsule he has taken (see YA-018).

## 5. Rules for moving objects
- **The Holding Rule:** An object moves only while Yethishwar is touching it.
- **The Persistence Rule:** When he lets go, the object freezes exactly where he left it. When time resumes, it stays in its new place.
- Yethishwar cannot "wake" any person or animal. A living being he carries remains frozen while carried and stays frozen where he sets it down.
- He can open unlocked doors, but cannot operate any machine, because electricity is frozen.

## 6. How time resumes
- **Automatically**, when the dial hand reaches the set duration, or
- **Early**, when Yethishwar presses the crown twice (the **Double-Press Resume**).
- At the moment of resumption, sound returns all at once, like a wave. Yethishwar calls the ringing in his ears afterward the **Resumption Ring**; it lasts up to 2 hours.
- The **Continuity Lock** re-joins Earth to the rest of the universe, so no shift in Earth's position or rotation can be measured.

## 7. What the world experiences
- **Nothing.** For every other person on Earth, no time passes. A sentence started at 12:00:00 is finished at 1:00:00 without a pause.
- Every clock on Earth was frozen too, so **no clock shows a missing hour.**
- People only notice if something was moved — for example, an object suddenly appearing in a different place.

## 8. Limitations
- **All-or-nothing:** It cannot stop time for only one person, one room or one city. It always stops the entire world.
- **Only Yethishwar can stay active.** No one else can be included in the Halo.
- **Maximum 60 minutes** per activation; **72-hour** recharge.
- **Yethishwar still ages** during the stop. Each stop makes him older than the rest of the world by its duration.
- **No Zero Clock inside the Wherever Door:** The Zero Clock will not activate while Yethishwar is away on a Wherever Door trip (see YA-018).
- **Bio-Key:** It will not work for anyone else.
