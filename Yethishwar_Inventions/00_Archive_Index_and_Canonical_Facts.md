## About this archive

The Yethishwar Archives is a **fictional** collection of research records, invention manuals, experiment logs, incident reports, and secrets belonging to one extraordinary inventor: **Yethishwar**, born in **Miryalaguda, Telangana, India**.

The archive does not describe a fictional planet or civilization. It describes one man, one secret laboratory, and four inventions that each challenge a fundamental rule of existence: the privacy of the mind, the flow of time, the needs of the body, and the limits of distance.

All inventions in this archive are fictional. None of them can be built, manufactured, or reproduced in the real world, and the archive contains no real-world instructions for doing so.

**Rule of the archive:** If a fact is not written in this archive, it is unknown. When two documents appear to disagree, this document (YA-000) is the official version.

## The story in brief

From childhood, Yethishwar was fascinated by questions that ordinary science could not answer. He wrote them down as **The Four Questions**:

1. Can one mind truly know another mind? (The Mind)
2. Can time stop for everyone except one person? (Time)
3. Must the human body depend on daily food and water? (The Body)
4. Can a person stand anywhere — in any place and any time — in a single step? (Place)

After years of secret research in **Laboratory Zero**, hidden beneath a disused rice mill on the outskirts of Miryalaguda, he built one invention to answer each question.

On **22 May 2026 at 12:00:00 PM IST**, Yethishwar activated the **Zero Clock** and stopped the entire world for **exactly 60 minutes**. He named that event **The Zero Hour**. It was only the beginning. He went on to complete the MindReader, the Eternal Nourishment Capsule, and the Wherever Door.

## The four inventions

| No. | Name | What it does | Conceived | Completed / first success |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | The MindReader | Reads another person's thoughts, mental images and memories | 2016 | Mark III operational 14 July 2026 |
| 002 | The Zero Clock | Stops time across the entire world | 2017 | ZC-7 completed 30 April 2026; first activation 22 May 2026 (The Zero Hour) |
| 003 | The Eternal Nourishment Capsule | One capsule sustains Yethishwar for 24 hours | 2018 | First successful 24-hour trial 1 September 2026 |

**Important:** Invention numbers follow the order in which each idea was **conceived**, not the order in which it was **completed**. The Zero Clock (002) was the first invention to work. The order of completion is: Zero Clock → MindReader → Nourishment Capsule → Wherever Door.

## Canonical facts (official version)

### Yethishwar
- Born **9 September 1999** in **Miryalaguda**, Nalgonda district, Telangana, India.
- Works alone in **Laboratory Zero**, established **March 2019**.
- Every invention is locked by a **Bio-Key**: it responds only to Yethishwar's biological signature.
- Because of the Zero Clock and the Wherever Door, Yethishwar's personal age is **6 hours and 59 minutes (419 minutes) older** than his calendar age (see YA-017).

### The MindReader (Invention 001)
- Two parts: the **Receiver Crown** (headset) and the **Focus Lens** (handheld).
- Activation word: **"Manas"**. Deactivation word: **"Close"**.
- Range: **maximum 10 metres**, with line of sight. One mind at a time.
- Four layers: Surface Thoughts, Mental Images, Recent Memory (last 7 days), Deep Memory (older than 7 days; requires touch).
- Maximum session **30 minutes**, cooldown **2 hours**, maximum **3 sessions per day**.
- Strictly **read-only**: it cannot plant, change, or erase thoughts.

### The Zero Clock (Invention 002)
- The Zero Hour: **22 May 2026, 12:00:00 PM IST to 1:00:00 PM IST** — exactly **60 minutes**.
- The entire Earth stopped. Yethishwar and the Zero Clock were unaffected.
- Duration per activation: **1 to 60 minutes**. Recharge after any activation: **72 hours**.
- Total global activations to date: **2** (the Zero Hour, 60 minutes; and Activation 2 on 20 July 2026, 5 minutes).
- Time resumes automatically when the set duration ends, or early by a **double-press of the crown**.

### The Eternal Nourishment Capsule (Invention 003)
- One capsule provides all nutrients, energy and hydration for **exactly 24 hours**.
- Works only for Yethishwar (Bio-Key). Inert in anyone else.
- Maximum **7 consecutive days**, then a **3-day Return Period** of normal food.
- Expires **90 days** after forging; colour changes from amber to grey.
- It is fictional and **cannot be manufactured from real-world ingredients or instructions**.

### The Wherever Door (Invention 004)
- Destination entered as **latitude and longitude in decimal degrees** (up to 6 decimal places).
- Temporal travel requires **date and time** in the format **YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS**, always in **IST (UTC+05:30)**.
- Temporal range: **1 January 1900 00:00:00 IST to 31 December 2099 23:59:59 IST**.
- The door **records the departure location and time automatically** (the **Anchor**).
- The **Return Key** brings the traveller back to the Anchor, arriving **1 minute after the original departure time**.
- Maximum trip: **72 hours** of personal time; then automatic **Recall**.
- The door can never open into **The Zero Hour** (the Frozen Hour Lock).
