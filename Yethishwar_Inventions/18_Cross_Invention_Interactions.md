# Cross-Invention Interactions

**Document ID:** YA-018
**Classification:** Official Rules (Canon)
**Related documents:** YA-005, YA-008, YA-012, YA-014

This document records what happens when two of Yethishwar's inventions are used together. These rules are canon.

---

## Interaction table
| Combination | Works? | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| MindReader + Zero Clock | Partly | Frozen minds show only the **Still Thought** |
| MindReader + Wherever Door | Yes | People in the past and the Probable Future can be read normally |
| MindReader + Capsule | Yes | No effect on each other |
| Zero Clock + Wherever Door | **No** | Zero Clock cannot activate during a door trip; door cannot open into a Zero Clock stop |
| Zero Clock + Capsule | Yes | Capsule counts Yethishwar's personal time during the stop |
| Wherever Door + Capsule | Yes | Capsule counts personal time on the trip, not the destination date |

---

## 1. MindReader + Zero Clock — The Still Thought
- **Discovered:** 20 July 2026, during Zero Clock Activation 2 (experiment MR-M3-04).
- When the world is stopped, every mind is stopped too. The MindReader cannot read thoughts that are not moving.
- Instead of thoughts, the MindReader shows **one single, motionless image**: the last thing the person was seeing, picturing or dreaming at the moment time stopped. This is called the **Still Thought**.
- Subject V-1's Still Thought was a riverbank at dawn — the dream V-1 was having when the stop began.
- Layers 2, 3 and 4 cannot be reached during stopped time. Memory cannot be read.
- The MindReader itself keeps working during the stop because Yethishwar holds it (Holding Rule).

## 2. MindReader + Wherever Door — Reading across time
- **Tested:** 25 October 2026 (MR-M3-12, trip T-004 to 1950).
- People in the past are real, living people. The MindReader reads them normally, with the same rules: 10 m range, consent, 30-minute limit.
- People in the **Probable Future** can also be read, but their thoughts belong to a future that may change.
- The Consent Code applies in every time period.

## 3. Zero Clock + Wherever Door — Forbidden together
- **The Frozen Hour Lock:** the Wherever Door can never open into a moment inside a Zero Clock stop. Forbidden windows: **22 May 2026 12:00:00–13:00:00 IST** and **20 July 2026 06:00:00–06:05:00 IST**. Any future stop is added automatically.
- **The Door Lock:** the Zero Clock cannot be activated while Yethishwar is away on a Wherever Door trip. Tested on 18 November 2026 at Everest Base Camp — the dial showed a small door symbol and refused.
- **Yethishwar cannot use the door during a stop:** the Wherever Door needs electricity and moving air in its frame, and both are frozen. The door stays locked while the world is stopped.
- **Reason (fictional):** the Stillstone holds time still and the Meridian Core folds time. Using both at once would tear the Continuity Lock.

## 4. Zero Clock + Capsule
- Yethishwar's body keeps working during a stop, so an active capsule keeps nourishing him.
- The capsule's 24 hours are counted in **personal time**. A 60-minute stop uses 60 minutes of the capsule.
- During the Zero Hour, Yethishwar was not using capsules yet (the first capsule trial was in August 2026).

## 5. Wherever Door + Capsule
- The capsule works in any place and any time period the door can reach.
- Its 24 hours are counted in **personal time**, not the calendar date at the destination. A capsule taken at 8:00 AM on 25 October 2026 kept working during the 3-hour visit to 26 January 1950.
- The capsule does **not** protect against heat, cold or altitude (confirmed at Everest Base Camp, C-09).

## 6. Rules that apply to every invention
- **Bio-Key:** every invention responds only to Yethishwar.
- **Dormant Lock:** if Laboratory Zero is forced open by anyone else, all inventions lock for 30 days.
- **Personal time:** Yethishwar ages normally during every stop and every trip. His total extra personal time is recorded in Sealed File S-03.
- **No profit, no harm:** no invention may be used for profit or to harm anyone.
