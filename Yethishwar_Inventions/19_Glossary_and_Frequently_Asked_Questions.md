## Part B — Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is Yethishwar?**
A fictional inventor born on 9 September 1999 in Miryalaguda, Telangana, India, who created the MindReader, the Zero Clock, the Eternal Nourishment Capsule and the Wherever Door.

**What is the Zero Hour?**
The 60 minutes on 22 May 2026, from 12:00:00 PM to 1:00:00 PM IST, when the Zero Clock stopped the entire world.

**Why did the Zero Hour start at exactly 12:00?**
Because the family wall clock stopped at 12:00 in 2007 (The Stopped Clock), which inspired the Zero Clock.

**Why did the Zero Hour last exactly 60 minutes?**
A new Stillstone must complete a full 60-minute Imprinting Cycle on its first use, and 60 minutes is also the maximum.

**Did anyone notice the Zero Hour?**
No. For everyone else no time passed and all clocks were frozen. The only trace is the Deep-Space Timing Gap (Sealed File S-02).

**Was anyone else unaffected by the Zero Hour?**
No. Only Yethishwar and the Zero Clock were unaffected.

**Did Yethishwar change anything during the Zero Hour?**
Yes, one thing: he moved a child out of the path of a lorry (Sealed File S-01). He also moved a small stone to test the Persistence Rule.

**How many times has the Zero Clock been used?**
Twice: the Zero Hour (60 minutes) and Activation 2 on 20 July 2026 (5 minutes).

**Can the MindReader read anyone's mind from far away?**
No. The maximum range is 10 metres, with line of sight, one person at a time.

**Can the MindReader change someone's thoughts?**
No. It is strictly read-only.

**Can the MindReader read Yethishwar's own mind or an animal's mind?**
No. His own mind returns nothing (Mirror Test); animals return only an Emotion Haze.

**How long can a MindReader session last?**
Maximum 30 minutes, then a 2-hour cooldown, with up to 3 sessions per day.

**What is the Eternal Nourishment Capsule made of?**
It is fictional and has no real ingredients. It cannot be manufactured using real-world ingredients or instructions.

**Can someone else take the capsule?**
It has no effect on anyone except Yethishwar (Bio-Key).

**What happens if Yethishwar takes two capsules?**
The second stays inert (Overlap Rejection). It does not give 48 hours.

**How long does a capsule last before expiring?**
90 days after forging. Expired capsules turn grey and are inert.

**How do you enter a destination in the Wherever Door?**
Latitude, then longitude, in decimal degrees with up to 6 decimal places. For time travel, also a date and time in YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS format, in IST.

**How does the traveller come back?**
Turn the Return Key twice in the air. The door appears within 10 seconds and returns the traveller to the Anchor, 1 minute after departure.

**What if the Return Key is lost?**
Automatic Recall brings the traveller back after 72 hours.

**Can the Wherever Door go to the Zero Hour?**
No. The Frozen Hour Lock forbids it (refused on 30 October 2026, trip T-005).

**Can Yethishwar meet his past self?**
No. The Self-Proximity Rule forbids destinations within 1 km and 24 hours of himself (his own birth was refused on 12 November 2026, trip T-007).

**How far into the past or future can the door go?**
From 1 January 1900 00:00:00 IST to 31 December 2099 23:59:59 IST.

**Can the Wherever Door take someone to the Moon?**
No. It can reach only the surface of the Earth.

**Has anyone else travelled through the door?**
No. The door allows 2 travellers, but no guest has ever travelled.

**How much older is Yethishwar than his calendar age?**
419 minutes — 6 hours and 59 minutes — as of 25 November 2026 (Sealed File S-03).

**What is Invention 005?**
Unknown. One page in notebook 47 says only: "The fifth question is the one I was afraid to ask."

**Who else knows about the inventions?**
No one knows about all of them. The two MindReader volunteers, Subject V-1 and Subject V-2, know only about the MindReader. Nobody else knows any invention exists.
