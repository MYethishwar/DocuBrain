## Prototype experiments (2020–2025)

### ZC-1 — The Brass Prototype
- **Year:** 2020
- **Design:** Brass case with a quartz core
- **Result:** No effect of any kind.
- **Lesson:** Ordinary crystals cannot hold time. Yethishwar begins searching for the crystal he later calls the **Stillstone**.

### ZC-2 — The Pendulum Test
- **Year:** 2021
- **Result:** A pendulum beside the device slowed by about 2% for 4 seconds.
- **Lesson:** Time can be "pressed" — the first proof the idea was possible.

### ZC-3 — The Cracked Core
- **Year:** 2022
- **Result:** The first raw Stillstone overheated and cracked after 9 seconds. Burn mark still visible on the Still Room workbench.
- **Lesson:** The Stillstone must be wound gradually, which led to the three-turn winding sequence.

### ZC-4 — The Candle Flame
- **Year:** 2023
- **Result:** A candle flame froze completely for **0.01 seconds**. Captured on a high-speed camera as a flame with no flicker.
- **Lesson:** A true stop is possible, but only for an instant.

### ZC-5 — The Fly in the Jar
- **Year:** 2024
- **Result:** A housefly inside a sealed glass jar froze in mid-air for **0.1 seconds**, then flew on unharmed.
- **Lesson:** Living things can be stopped and resume safely.

### ZC-6 — The Water Droplet
- **Date:** 11 November 2025
- **Result:** A falling water droplet froze in mid-air for **0.3 seconds** inside a **1-metre** field. Yethishwar touched the droplet; it moved with his finger and froze again when he pulled away — the first evidence of the **Holding Rule** and **Persistence Rule**.
- **Problem:** The field could not be made larger than 1 metre or longer than 0.3 seconds.
- **Lesson:** A small field is unstable. Yethishwar concluded the field must be **all-or-nothing** — the entire world, or nothing.

### ZC-7 — The Final Zero Clock
- **Completed:** 30 April 2026
- **Design:** Silver stopwatch, 52 mm, mature Stillstone core, Halo Emitter, Continuity Lock and Bio-Key Plate
- **Testing problem:** Because the field is all-or-nothing, ZC-7 could not be tested on a small scale. Its very first activation would have to stop the entire world, and the Imprinting Cycle required it to last 60 minutes.
- **Preparation:** 22 days of checks (30 April – 21 May 2026).

## Global activations (ZC-7)

### Activation 1 — The Zero Hour
- **Date:** 22 May 2026
- **Start:** 12:00:00 PM IST (Auto-Start Timer)
- **End:** 1:00:00 PM IST (automatic)
- **Duration:** 60 minutes (Imprinting Cycle)
- **Result:** Complete success. The entire world stopped. See YA-009.

### Activation 2 — The Still Thought test
- **Date:** 20 July 2026
- **Start:** 6:00:00 AM IST
- **End:** 6:05:00 AM IST (automatic)
- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Purpose:** Test whether the MindReader can read a mind frozen in stopped time.
- **Result:** The frozen mind of Subject V-1 returned only a single motionless image — the **Still Thought**. See YA-006 (MR-M3-04) and YA-018.
- **Additional observation:** At 6:00 AM, the sunrise light over Miryalaguda froze as a band of orange across the sky. Birds in the dawn chorus were frozen mid-song.

### Refused activation — The Door Lock test
- **Date:** 18 November 2026, during Wherever Door trip T-008 (Everest Base Camp)
- **Result:** The Zero Clock refused to activate. Its dial showed a small door symbol. Confirms the rule that the Zero Clock cannot be activated during a Wherever Door trip. This does **not** count as an activation, and no recharge was used.
