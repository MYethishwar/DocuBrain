# Invention 001 — The MindReader: Operating Rules and Limitations

**Document ID:** YA-005
**Classification:** Official Rules (Canon)
**Invention number:** 001
**Related documents:** YA-004 (Technical Manual), YA-006 (Experiment Log), YA-016 (Incidents)

These are the official rules of the MindReader. If another document disagrees, YA-000 and this document are correct.

---

## 1. Range
- **Maximum range:** 10 metres from the Focus Lens to the target's head. At 10.1 metres and beyond, the reading fails.
- **Line of sight is required.** Yethishwar must be able to see the target's head.
- **Works through:** clear or tinted glass, thin cloth, thin wooden doors (under 5 cm), fog and darkness (if he can still aim).
- **Blocked by:** any metal sheet, concrete or brick walls thicker than 30 cm, and water deeper than 1 metre between the Lens and the target.

## 2. Activation
- Only Yethishwar can activate it (**Bio-Key**).
- Activation requires the Receiver Crown, the Focus Lens trigger, and the spoken word **"Manas."**
- Deactivation: the word **"Close,"** or releasing the trigger for more than 5 seconds.

## 3. Whose thoughts can be read
- **Any awake human being aged 2 years or older,** of any language, within range.
- **Only one person at a time.** In a crowd, the Lens locks onto the single head at the centre of its aim.
- **Cannot be read:**
  - **Yethishwar himself** — pointing the Lens at a mirror produces nothing (the "Mirror Test," July 2026).
  - **Animals** — produce only a vague feeling called the **Emotion Haze** (for example, contentment or fear), never thoughts or images.
  - **Infants under 2 years** — their thoughts are too unstructured to convert.
  - **Sleeping people** — Layers 1 to 3 return only broken dream fragments. Layer 4 (Deep Memory) also fails during sleep.
  - **People frozen by the Zero Clock** — return only a single motionless image called the **Still Thought** (see YA-018).

## 4. Memory access
- **Layer 3 (Recent Memory):** memories from the last **7 days** only.
- **Layer 4 (Deep Memory):** memories older than 7 days, requiring **physical touch** and a **calm, consenting** target. Limited to **10 minutes** within any session.
- Memories appear as Yethishwar would see them through the target's eyes, including feelings, but not always in perfect order.
- Memories the target has truly forgotten appear blurred or silent.

## 5. Time limits
- **Maximum session:** 30 minutes. At 25 minutes the status light turns amber as a warning.
- **Cooldown:** 2 hours after each session.
- **Maximum per day:** 3 sessions.
- **Battery:** 4 hours per Crystal Cell; 6 hours to recharge.

## 6. Read-only rule
The MindReader is **strictly read-only**. It **cannot**:
- place a thought into anyone's mind,
- change, erase or create a memory,
- control a person's actions,
- make a person aware of Yethishwar's thoughts.

## 7. Effects on the target
- Most targets feel nothing.
- About **1 in 5 people** feel a faint, warm pressure behind the eyes, which disappears when the session ends.
- No target has ever been harmed.

## 8. Effects on Yethishwar (side effects)
- **The Echo:** after a session, Yethishwar may hear faint traces of the target's inner voice for up to 1 hour. Sessions longer than 30 minutes cause much longer Echoes (see the 28 July 2026 overload, YA-016).
- **Echo headache:** a mild headache after Layer 4 sessions.

## 9. The Consent Code
Yethishwar wrote three rules for himself, carved on the MindReader's storage case:
1. **Read only with consent,** except to prevent immediate harm to a person.
2. **Never reveal what was read** to anyone else.
3. **Never use a reading for profit, advantage or revenge.**
