# Invention 001 — The MindReader: Technical Manual

**Document ID:** YA-004
**Classification:** Invention Manual
**Invention number:** 001
**Category:** Mind-reading machine
**Related documents:** YA-005 (Rules and Limitations), YA-006 (Experiment Log), YA-018 (Cross-Invention Interactions)

---

## Key facts
- **Purpose:** Allows Yethishwar to perceive another person's thoughts, mental images and memories.
- **Fictional principle:** It converts neural thought patterns into a readable **mental projection** that appears inside Yethishwar's own mind.
- **Conceived:** 2016 (the first of the Four Questions — "Can one mind truly know another mind?")
- **Current model:** Mark III, fully operational since **14 July 2026**
- **Operator:** Yethishwar only (Bio-Key locked)
- **Activation word:** "Manas" (meaning "mind")
- **Deactivation word:** "Close"

---

## Components

### 1. The Receiver Crown
A thin, silver-grey headband worn across the forehead and temples. It contains:
- **Temple pads** on both sides that deliver the mental projection directly into Yethishwar's perception.
- A **Bio-Key strip** across the forehead that confirms the wearer is Yethishwar. If anyone else wears it, the Crown stays dark.
- A small **status light** above the right temple: blue = ready, green = reading, amber = near time limit, red = cooldown.

### 2. The Focus Lens
A palm-sized, round lens of smoky glass set in a brass ring, with a trigger under the thumb. Yethishwar points the Lens at the target's head. The Lens selects exactly **one** mind and "locks" onto it.

### 3. The Crystal Cell
The power source, fitted inside the Focus Lens handle. One fully charged Crystal Cell provides **4 hours** of operation and takes **6 hours** to recharge.

---

## How it works (fictional principle)

Every thought produces a faint, unique pattern of neural activity. The archive calls this pattern the **thought-signature**. The MindReader works in three stages:

1. **Capture** — The Focus Lens gathers the target's thought-signature across the air, up to a maximum distance of 10 metres.
2. **Conversion** — Inside the Lens, the **Semantic Converter** turns the thought-signature into pure meaning. Meaning has no language, so the MindReader works on speakers of any language.
3. **Projection** — The Receiver Crown projects that meaning into Yethishwar's mind as a **mental projection**: he "hears" inner speech as a quiet voice, and "sees" mental images as translucent pictures, as if remembering them himself.

Nothing appears on a screen. Nothing is recorded by the device. The only record of a reading is what Yethishwar writes in his log afterwards.

## The four reading layers

| Layer | Name | What Yethishwar perceives | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Surface Thoughts | The target's present inner speech — what they are thinking right now | Line of sight, up to 10 m |
| L2 | Mental Images | Pictures the target is imagining or picturing right now | Line of sight, up to 10 m |
| L3 | Recent Memory | Memories from the last 7 days | Line of sight, up to 10 m; target must be awake |
| L4 | Deep Memory | Memories older than 7 days, back to early childhood | Physical touch (hand on the target's shoulder or arm) and a calm target; maximum 10 minutes per session |

Yethishwar moves between layers by sliding his thumb along the Focus Lens ring: one click per layer.

## Activation sequence
1. Put on the Receiver Crown. The Bio-Key strip confirms Yethishwar (status light turns blue).
2. Point the Focus Lens at the target, within 10 metres and with clear line of sight.
3. Press and hold the thumb trigger.
4. Say the activation word **"Manas."** The status light turns green and the reading begins at Layer 1.
5. To end the session, say **"Close"** or release the trigger for more than 5 seconds.
