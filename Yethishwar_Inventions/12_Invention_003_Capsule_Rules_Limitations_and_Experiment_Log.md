# Invention 003 — The Eternal Nourishment Capsule: Rules, Limitations and Experiment Log

**Document ID:** YA-012
**Classification:** Official Rules (Canon) and Experiment Log
**Invention number:** 003
**Related documents:** YA-011 (Technical Manual), YA-016 (Incidents), YA-018 (Interactions)

The Eternal Nourishment Capsule is fictional. It cannot be manufactured using real-world ingredients or instructions.

---

## Part A — Official rules

### 1. Duration
- One capsule lasts **exactly 24 hours** from the moment it is swallowed.
- The 24 hours are counted in **Yethishwar's personal time**, not calendar time (this matters during the Zero Clock and Wherever Door trips; see YA-018).

### 2. Fictional energy source
- The **Prana Field**, converted by the **Annam Lattice** through **Impossible Matter Conversion**.

### 3. Effects
- No hunger or thirst for 24 hours (onset after 15 minutes).
- Steady energy and a clear mind throughout the day.
- Normal body temperature and normal sleep.
- Body waste reduced by about 90%.
- **The Bell** — a mild hunger signal at hour 23.

### 4. Who it works for
- **Only Yethishwar** (Bio-Key). In anyone else, the capsule is inert and has no effect at all.

### 5. One per day — the Overlap Rejection rule
- Only one capsule can be active at a time. A second capsule swallowed during an active capsule is rejected and stays inert. Taking two does **not** give 48 hours or double energy.

### 6. The Seven-Day Rule and Return Period
- Maximum **7 consecutive days** on capsules.
- After 7 days, Yethishwar must eat normal food for at least **3 days** — the **Return Period** — so his digestive system stays active.

### 7. Limitations
- **No taste or pleasure:** it does not replace the enjoyment of meals.
- **No healing:** it does not cure illness, injury or tiredness from lack of sleep.
- **No extra strength:** it supplies normal needs only, not superhuman energy.
- **The Exertion Gap:** after more than 6 hours of very heavy physical effort, the capsule may fall short and Yethishwar may feel hunger before hour 23.
- **Crushing destroys it:** a crushed or broken capsule loses its glow and becomes useless.

### 8. Expiry
- Each capsule expires **90 days** after its forge date.
- An expired capsule changes colour from **amber to grey** and stops glowing.
- An expired capsule is inert and harmless but gives no nourishment.

### 9. Storage
- Below **40°C**, in darkness, away from strong magnets.
- Stored in a teak box lined with black cloth, in the Annam Forge room.

## Part B — Experiment log

All times IST.

### C-01 — First short trial
- **Date:** 18 August 2026
- **Result:** Capsule ENC-0003 gave full nourishment for 6 hours before Yethishwar ended the test by eating lunch. No side effects.

### C-02 — First full 24-hour trial
- **Date:** 1 September 2026, 6:00 AM, to 2 September 2026, 6:00 AM
- **Capsule:** ENC-0011
- **Result:** **Complete success.** No food or water for 24 hours. Onset at 6:15 AM. The Bell at 5:00 AM the next morning. Weight, pulse and blood pressure unchanged.

### C-03 — Overlap test
- **Date:** 5 September 2026
- **Result:** A second capsule (ENC-0016) taken 3 hours after the first stayed inert. Confirms **Overlap Rejection**.

### C-04 — Bio-Key test
- **Date:** 9 September 2026
- **Result:** Capsule ENC-0019 placed in a sealed digestion simulator. It stayed inert and glowed dimly for 24 hours, then faded. Confirms the capsule works only for Yethishwar.

### C-05 — Crush test
- **Date:** 14 September 2026
- **Result:** Capsule ENC-0024 crushed in a vice. Glow vanished instantly; the Lattice became grey dust. Confirms crushing destroys it.

### C-06 — Heavy exertion test
- **Date:** 21 September 2026
- **Result:** After 7 hours of heavy work moving equipment in Laboratory Zero, Yethishwar felt hunger at hour 19. Confirms the **Exertion Gap**. See IR-06 in YA-016.

### C-07 — The Seven-Day Streak
- **Dates:** 1 October 2026, 6:00 AM, to 8 October 2026, 6:00 AM
- **Result:** Success. 7 capsules, 7 days, no food or water. On day 7, Yethishwar noticed his stomach felt "quiet." He began the 3-day Return Period on 8 October with rice and dal.

### C-08 — Capsule during time travel
- **Date:** 25 October 2026 (Wherever Door trip T-004 to 1950)
- **Result:** The capsule continued working normally in the past. Its 24 hours were counted in Yethishwar's personal time.

### C-09 — Capsule at high altitude and cold
- **Date:** 18 November 2026 (Wherever Door trip T-008 to Everest Base Camp)
- **Result:** Capsule worked normally at 5,364 m and below freezing. Yethishwar felt no hunger, though he still felt cold — the capsule does not protect against temperature.

### C-10 — Expiry check
- **Date:** 20 November 2026
- **Result:** The earliest capsules forged in August had turned grey. All grey capsules were inert. Confirms the 90-day expiry.
