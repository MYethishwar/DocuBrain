# Invention 004 — The Wherever Door: Technical Manual

**Document ID:** YA-013
**Classification:** Invention Manual
**Invention number:** 004
**Category:** Space and time transportation
**Related documents:** YA-014 (Coordinate and Travel Rules), YA-015 (Travel Log), YA-018 (Interactions)

## Key facts
- **Purpose:** Transports a person to any location on Earth by entering **latitude and longitude**.
- **Time travel:** Also accepts a **date and time**, allowing travel to a specified point in the past or future.
- **Return feature:** The door **automatically records the origin** (the Anchor) and can bring the traveller back with the **Return Key**.
- **Conceived:** 2019 (the fourth of the Four Questions — "Can a person stand anywhere, in any place and any time, in a single step?")
- **Completed:** **10 October 2026**
- **First trip:** **17 October 2026** — Taj Mahal, Agra (Present Mode)
- **Home position:** **Anchor Alpha**, the centre of the Door Hall in Laboratory Zero
- **Operator:** Yethishwar only (Bio-Key)

---

## Physical description
- A freestanding door in its own frame, standing on a stone base. It is not attached to any wall.
- **Frame:** seasoned teak wood, carved with lines of latitude and longitude.
- **Door:** painted deep **indigo blue**, 2.1 metres tall and 0.9 metres wide.
- **Handle:** brass, with a Bio-Key plate in the grip.
- **Coordinate Panel:** a brass panel on the right side of the frame with a numeric keypad, a +/− key, a small amber display, and three buttons: **SET**, **CLEAR** and **OPEN**.
- **The Return Key:** a heavy brass key, 9 cm long, on a leather cord, with a compass rose on its bow. It has no matching keyhole.
- **Meridian Core:** the fictional power source, hidden inside the top of the frame.

## How it works (fictional principle)

The Wherever Door works by **Threshold Folding**:

1. **Entry:** Yethishwar enters coordinates (and, for time travel, a date and time) on the Coordinate Panel and presses **SET**.
2. **Safe-Arrival Check:** The door scans the destination. If the destination is unsafe or forbidden, the display shows a red message and the door stays locked.
3. **Folding:** If safe, the **Meridian Core** folds the space (and time) between the doorway and the destination, so that the doorway and the destination become one threshold.
4. **Crossing:** Yethishwar presses **OPEN** and steps through. On the other side he stands on the ground at the destination.
5. **Anchor recording:** At the moment of departure, the door **automatically records** the latitude, longitude, date and time of departure. This is the **Anchor**.
6. **Closing:** Behind him, the doorway closes and disappears at the destination. At Laboratory Zero, the door stays standing and its display reads **TRAVELLER AWAY**.

## Display messages
| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| READY | Door is charged and waiting for coordinates |
| SAFE — PRESS OPEN | Destination passed the Safe-Arrival Check |
| UNSAFE: SOLID | Destination is inside solid matter |
| UNSAFE: WATER | Destination is in water deeper than 1 metre |
| UNSAFE: TERRAIN | Destination is a slope steeper than 45°, fire or lava |
| OUT OF RANGE: TIME | Date is before 1900 or after 2099 |
| FROZEN HOUR LOCK | Date and time fall inside a Zero Clock stop |
| SELF-PROXIMITY | Another Yethishwar is within 1 km and 24 hours of the destination |
| TRAVELLER AWAY | A trip is in progress |
| RECHARGING | Daily trip limit reached |

## The Return Key
- To return, Yethishwar holds the Return Key in the air and turns it **twice clockwise**.
- Within **10 seconds**, the outline of the indigo door appears in front of him at his current location.
- He steps through and arrives back at the **Anchor** — exactly **1 minute after his original departure time**, no matter how long the trip lasted. This is the **One-Minute Return**.
- The Return Key also works as a beacon. If it is lost or the traveller cannot use it, **Recall** happens automatically after 72 hours.
