## IR-01 — The Cracked Core
- **Date:** 2022
- **Invention:** Zero Clock prototype ZC-3
- **What happened:** The first raw Stillstone overheated after 9 seconds and cracked with a loud snap. The workbench was scorched.
- **Injuries:** A small burn on Yethishwar's left palm, healed in two weeks.
- **Outcome:** Introduced the three-turn winding sequence. The burn mark is still visible on the Still Room workbench.

## IR-02 — The Crossing
- **Date:** 22 May 2026, 12:24 PM (during the Zero Hour)
- **Invention:** Zero Clock
- **What happened:** Yethishwar moved a frozen child out of the path of a frozen lorry. This was the only intervention of the Zero Hour.
- **Outcome:** Full details are sealed. See **Sealed File S-01** in YA-017.

## IR-03 — Resumption Ring and exhaustion
- **Date:** 22 May 2026, 1:00 PM onwards
- **Invention:** Zero Clock
- **What happened:** After time resumed, Yethishwar experienced ringing in his ears for about 2 hours and slept 11 hours that night.
- **Outcome:** Named the **Resumption Ring**. After Activation 2 (5 minutes), the ringing lasted only 10 minutes. Rule noted: the longer the stop, the longer the Ring.

## IR-04 — The Six-Hour Echo
- **Date:** 28 July 2026
- **Invention:** MindReader Mark III
- **What happened:** During experiment MR-M3-07, Yethishwar ignored the amber warning light and continued reading Subject V-1 for **41 minutes** — 11 minutes over the 30-minute limit.
- **Effects:** For **6 hours** afterwards he heard V-1's inner voice as an Echo, mixed with his own thoughts, and had a severe headache. He could not sleep until the Echo faded.
- **Outcome:** The 30-minute limit became absolute. A forced shut-off was added: at 30 minutes the Receiver Crown now turns red and stops by itself.

## IR-05 — Warm-pressure report
- **Date:** 12 September 2026
- **Invention:** MindReader Mark III
- **What happened:** Subject V-2 reported a warm pressure behind the eyes during the language test (MR-M3-11).
- **Outcome:** Faded within one minute of the session ending. Consistent with the known effect felt by about 1 in 5 people. No harm.

## IR-06 — The Exertion Gap
- **Date:** 21 September 2026
- **Invention:** Eternal Nourishment Capsule
- **What happened:** After 7 hours of heavy work, Yethishwar felt hunger at hour 19 instead of hour 23.
- **Outcome:** He ate a small meal. Confirmed the **Exertion Gap**: the capsule may fall short after more than 6 hours of very heavy effort.

## IR-07 — The Lost Key at Everest
- **Date:** 18 November 2026 (trip T-008)
- **Invention:** Wherever Door
- **What happened:** Yethishwar's gloved hands were numb from the cold. The Return Key slipped off its cord into soft snow. He searched for **4 minutes** before finding it by the glint of its brass compass rose.
- **Risk:** Without the key, he would have had to wait for automatic Recall after 72 hours, at 5,364 metres, in freezing conditions.
- **Outcome:** New rule — the Return Key is now worn on a steel chain around the neck, never on a cord.

## IR-08 — Future Fade of the neem leaf
- **Date:** 4 November 2026 (trip T-006)
- **Invention:** Wherever Door
- **What happened:** A neem leaf picked in Miryalaguda in the year 2050 dissolved into fine green light **52 minutes** after Yethishwar returned.
- **Outcome:** Confirmed **Future Fade**: objects from the future dissolve within 1 hour. Objects from the past (like the 1950 paper flag) stay.

## IR-09 — Cargo landing error
- **Date:** 1 October 2026 (before the door was completed)
- **Invention:** Wherever Door
- **What happened:** In the first cargo test, a 10 kg rice bag was sent to the mill yard above Laboratory Zero. It arrived **2 metres** away from the entered coordinates.
- **Outcome:** The Meridian Core was recalibrated. All later trips arrived within 0.11 metres of the target. The door was declared complete on 10 October 2026.

## Anomalies (unexplained observations)

### A-01 — The Deep-Space Timing Gap
After the Zero Hour, a deep-space probe far outside the Stillness Field showed a timing irregularity that ground stations logged as an equipment fault. Details in **Sealed File S-02** (YA-017).

### A-02 — The Still Thought
A mind frozen by the Zero Clock shows only one motionless image. Why this image is always the last thing the person saw or imagined is not understood. See YA-018.

### A-03 — The Permitted Birthday
The Wherever Door allowed a trip to Miryalaguda on Yethishwar's 51st birthday in 2050, meaning no other Yethishwar was within 1 km. The reason is unknown. See **Sealed File S-04** (YA-017).
